# Business Knowledge AI — Three UI/UX Concepts

> **Status:** These are visual mockups only. The React frontend, backend/API behavior, retrieval pipeline, LangGraph graph, and data model are unchanged. Select **A**, **B**, or **C** before any frontend implementation begins.

## OPTION A — Dark Executive

![Dark Executive visual mockup](/manus-storage/business-knowledge-ai-option-a-dark-executive_76e77b82.png)

The Dark Executive concept treats Business Knowledge AI as an executive research and decision-support product. Its near-black surface, warm gold evidence markers, executive navigation, and structured **Evidence Ledger** signal high-value intelligence work rather than casual conversation.

| Design element | Direction |
| --- | --- |
| Color palette | Charcoal `#101214`, graphite panels, warm gold `#C79A46`, off-white text, muted grays |
| Layout | Executive navigation rail, focused central research canvas, permanent right Evidence Ledger |
| Chat composition | Concise answer cards with restrained inline citations and a premium research composer |
| Source presentation | Ledger-style citation cards with chapter, page, excerpt, and official-source action |
| Typography | High-contrast sans-serif hierarchy with large, confident research headings |
| Interaction pattern | Evidence-first review, with the source ledger acting as a durable audit trail |

This option fits Business Knowledge AI when the portfolio should emphasize **corporate credibility, managerial analysis, and premium SaaS polish**. It is the strongest choice for consulting, strategy, or executive-training audiences.

## OPTION B — Modern Blue Intelligence

![Modern Blue Intelligence visual mockup](/manus-storage/business-knowledge-ai-option-b-modern-blue-intelligence_ca5079e2.png)

Modern Blue Intelligence frames the product as a contemporary enterprise knowledge platform. A deep navy foundation, electric-blue actions, structured top navigation, and a dedicated knowledge-source panel give it a technical and operational identity.

| Design element | Direction |
| --- | --- |
| Color palette | Deep navy `#071C3D`, electric blue `#2E7BFF`, soft cyan `#69E7FF`, white and cool gray |
| Layout | Top product navigation, stacked left history rail, broad central answer timeline, tall source drawer |
| Chat composition | Structured intelligence cards, visible status badges, and a docked command-style composer |
| Source presentation | Vertical retrieval stack with source coverage, metadata, excerpts, and official links |
| Typography | Dense but legible enterprise hierarchy using a modern sans-serif system |
| Interaction pattern | Fast scanning through tabs, filters, evidence status, and a multi-signal response timeline |

This option fits Business Knowledge AI when the portfolio should demonstrate **technical product design, evidence transparency, and enterprise AI fluency**. It provides the clearest visual home for future research-library and activity features.

## OPTION C — Light Editorial Knowledge

![Light Editorial Knowledge visual mockup](/manus-storage/business-knowledge-ai-option-c-light-editorial-knowledge_ddd45d34.png)

Light Editorial Knowledge makes the product feel like a premium annotated course reader. A warm ivory canvas, deep evergreen controls, terracotta annotation cues, serif display typography, and an **Evidence Margin** make the source material feel central to the learning experience.

| Design element | Direction |
| --- | --- |
| Color palette | Ivory `#F8F4EA`, evergreen `#184C3A`, terracotta `#C86B4A`, charcoal `#22211E` |
| Layout | Notebook-style conversation rail, expansive reading canvas, editorial evidence margin |
| Chat composition | Answer as a well-typeset study note with inline superscript citations and a key-takeaway pull quote |
| Source presentation | Paper-like evidence excerpts organized by chapter and page, rather than generic cards |
| Typography | Editorial serif display type paired with disciplined sans-serif controls |
| Interaction pattern | Read, annotate, inspect evidence, and continue studying through chapter-aware prompts |

This option fits Business Knowledge AI when the portfolio should emphasize **learning quality, academic trust, and a distinctive knowledge-product identity**. It best expresses the OpenStax textbook grounding without looking like a generic AI chat UI.

## Selection guidance

| Option | Best for | Distinctive strength | Main tradeoff |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **A — Dark Executive** | Executive/business portfolio positioning | Premium strategic-analysis tone | Less naturally academic |
| **B — Modern Blue Intelligence** | Enterprise AI and technical-product positioning | Strongest information hierarchy and future scalability | Most familiar enterprise pattern |
| **C — Light Editorial Knowledge** | Research, study, and source-grounded product positioning | Most ownable textbook-research identity | Requires disciplined typography and whitespace |

**Recommended choice: Option C.** It most clearly differentiates Business Knowledge AI through its textbook evidence and turns citations into a memorable research experience. Choose **A**, **B**, or **C** to authorize implementation. Until then, no UI code will be changed.
